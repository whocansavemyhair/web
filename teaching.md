      <h2 id="societal-course-title">AI &amp; Data Science for Societal Systems</h2>
      <dl class="teaching-details">
        <div><dt>Instructor</dt><dd>Dr. Hao Frank Yang</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="teaching-description">Build AI systems that turn data into better decisions for society. Using transportation and public health as recurring case studies, this course connects sensing and databases with machine learning, spatio-temporal modeling, computer vision, multimodal learning, and generative AI. Students develop a complete data-to-decision pipeline while examining uncertainty, privacy, fairness, interpretability, and responsible deployment.</p>
